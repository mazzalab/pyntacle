Mesoscale Metrics
=================

Mesoscale metrics capture **intermediate-range organization** in a network: they are more informative than purely local descriptors (degree-like measures), but less coarse than global indices (diameter, density, etc.).
In biological networks, these measures are often used to quantify *neighborhood similarity* and *multi-step influence/propagation*.
